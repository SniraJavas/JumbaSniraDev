I am a South African fullstack software engineer with 8+ years of experience building and maintaining software in C#, .NET and Azure, with modern JavaScript frameworks on the front end. I have delivered solutions in media, government, logistics, insurance and education.

I am happy on both legacy and modern codebases. I like to get up to speed fast, improve readability and performance as I go, and work with support and business teams so production issues are fixed and stay fixed. Recent work: At MultiChoice (2025–2026) I worked across several software divisions, strengthening SDLC practices and deploying through Azure Pipelines and Jenkins. At Tipp Focus I consulted on National Treasury's Central Supplier Database (2024–2025), and at Genii Analytics AI I built a custom Azure API-based tool that turned per-client SaaS deployments into a single-click process (2022–2024).

Before that: I built Azure Service Bus microservices and Azure Functions for an insurance integration project, upgraded a logistics platform from Angular 1 to Angular 12, introduced Azure Pipelines CI/CD for a lending platform, and started my career building insurance and education applications in C# and .NET MVC.

I was born and raised in the village of Tabase in Umtata, Eastern Cape, and hold a BSc in Computer Science from the University of the Western Cape.
